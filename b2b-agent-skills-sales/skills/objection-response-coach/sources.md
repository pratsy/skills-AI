# Sources and references

## Objection Response Coach

### Relevance

This skill reflects common sales enablement and negotiation methods used to resolve buyer objections without sounding defensive or generic.

### Example references

- sales objection handling frameworks used in enterprise sales training
- negotiation and buyer-resistance analysis models
- B2B sales coaching methodologies for objection management
- buyer psychology and risk-reduction models in solution selling

### Notes

The goal is to help reps respond with business logic, empathy, and deal progression rather than canned rebuttals.
