# Objection Response Coach

## Purpose

Generate high-quality objection handling responses for sales reps based on common customer concerns, buying context, and deal stage.

## Business problem

Sales reps often respond to objections with generic answers that do not address the buyer's actual risk, urgency, or buying criteria.

## Inputs

- objection summary
- buyer persona
- deal stage
- customer context
- competitive context

## Outputs

- objection response options
- recommended answer structure
- confidence and risk framing
- follow-up question suggestions

## Use cases

- discovery follow-up
- proposal conversations
- pricing or ROI objections
- stakeholder pushback
- competitive comparison moments

## Prompt

“You are a senior B2B sales coach. The rep is dealing with this objection from a buyer: [objection]. The buyer profile is [persona], the deal stage is [stage], and the context is [context]. Suggest 3 strong objection-handling response options. Each response should: acknowledge the concern, address the buyer’s risk, link the solution to business value, and create a natural next step. Also suggest a follow-up question to uncover deeper context.”

## Example output

- Response option 1: validate the concern, reframe around business risk, emphasize measurable ROI
- Response option 2: address operational friction, reduce perceived implementation burden, align to milestone-based rollout
- Response option 3: redirect to stakeholder priorities, tie the investment to speed, team efficiency, or conversion improvement

## Evaluation

- Response addresses the real concern rather than sounding scripted
- It includes business value and risk reduction
- It creates a next step instead of a dead-end rebuttal
- It is adapted to the buying context and stage
