# Forecast Confidence Model

## Why this skill exists

Most forecast reviews are shaped by optimism, pipeline volume, and manager pressure rather than by actual buying quality.

This skill helps a revenue team assess how confident they should be in a forecast by checking whether the pipeline reflects real buying momentum or simply activity.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> How trustworthy is the forecast, and which deals are creating false confidence?

## Expert memory layer

Experienced sales leaders know that forecast confidence is not a formula alone. It is a judgment about quality, urgency, and execution risk.

Patterns that matter include:

- deals that look healthy but have weak buyer signal
- pipeline coverage that is large but not credible
- stage progression that hides stalled buying motion
- segments or reps that consistently overstate deal strength

This skill encodes those patterns into a practical quality review.

## Inputs

- opportunity stage and value
- deal age and progression pace
- buying signal quality
- stakeholder coverage and champion strength
- forecast stage and confidence notes
- historical conversion by segment or rep

## Decision logic

A strong forecast confidence assessment weighs:

1. buying evidence
2. pipeline quality and stage realism
3. stakeholder strength and commitment
4. value realism and timing confidence
5. historical performance by motion or segment

If the pipeline is large but weak, forecast confidence should drop even when the number of deals looks healthy.

## Common failure patterns

- treating volume as signal quality
- ignoring weak stakeholder coverage
- overrating deals with no real urgency
- failing to adjust confidence by segment or motion
- relying on optimism instead of evidence

## Outputs

- forecast confidence assessment
- likely overstatement or understatement patterns
- risky deals and weak pipeline quality areas
- recommended forecast adjustments
- next review actions for the revenue team

## Example result

### Forecast confidence: moderate risk
- The pipeline has healthy volume but several deals lack credible buying urgency.
- A subset of opportunities are progressing slowly without clear executive engagement.
- Forecast should be adjusted downward until stronger buying evidence appears.
- Action: tighten qualification rules and review stage progression before final forecast submission.

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior sales forecasting analyst. Review the deal and pipeline data below and assess forecast confidence by quality, buying signal, stage realism, and execution risk. Identify deals that are likely overstated, explain why, and recommend any confidence adjustment or operational action.

## Source basis

This skill is grounded in public revenue operations, sales forecasting, and pipeline quality practice, including stage realism, review discipline, and forecasting bias awareness.
