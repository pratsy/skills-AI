# Executive Sponsor Identifier

## Purpose

Find the most influential buyer or internal champion for a deal by analyzing stakeholder map, buying committee structure, and influence patterns.

## Business problem

Sales teams often focus too much on the direct contact and not enough on the economic buyer, technical validator, or executive sponsor. This creates weak deal momentum and poor strategic alignment.

## Inputs

- account details
- stakeholder list
- call notes
- CRM relationship data
- deal stage and competitive context

## Outputs

- likely executive sponsor
- buying committee map
- influence and risk assessment
- recommended engagement strategy

## Use cases

- strategic deals
- enterprise expansion
- multi-stakeholder sales cycles
- cross-functional account planning

## Prompt

“You are a sales strategy advisor. Analyze the stakeholder map and opportunity context to identify the most likely executive sponsor, buying committee members, and key leverage points. Recommend who to engage, what message to use, and how to align with the buying process.”

## Example output

- Executive sponsor candidate: VP of Operations
- Influence drivers: budget ownership, cross-functional impact, strategic urgency
- Recommended next move: align value narrative to operational efficiency and risk reduction

## Evaluation

- Sponsor recommendation is consistent with buying power and strategic context
- Buyer map is realistic and useful to the rep
- Guidance is specific enough to drive outreach and deal planning
