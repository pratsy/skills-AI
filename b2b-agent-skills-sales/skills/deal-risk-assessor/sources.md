### Relevance

This skill is informed by common deal review, pipeline health, and forecasting practices used in sales operations and enterprise sales management.

### Example references

- pipeline health analysis in B2B sales organizations
- forecast reliability and deal quality frameworks

### Notes

The goal is to support better decision-making in deal reviews without overcomplicating the risk model.
