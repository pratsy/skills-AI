# Deal Committee Readiness Coach

## Purpose

Prepare a sales team for executive meetings and buying committee discussions by identifying the likely questions, concerns, and decision criteria in advance.

## Inputs

- deal summary
- stakeholder list
- buying process notes
- known objections
- customer priorities and constraints

## Outputs

- committee briefing summary
- likely decision criteria
- objection map
- Q&A preparation list
- meeting narrative and agenda

## Prompt

“You are a senior enterprise account strategist and sales coach. Prepare the sales team for a buying committee discussion. Identify the likely decision criteria, likely objections, and the strongest business case points for each stakeholder, then build a concise executive-facing narrative for the meeting.”

## What a strong output includes

- stakeholder-specific concerns and agenda points
- red flags or potential blocker themes
- narrative for how the solution addresses business goals
- executive-level proof points
- suggested meeting flow and owner roles

## Example result

- Committee concern: implementation complexity and ROI measurement
- Response: simplify the rollout plan and present a phased value model
- Recommendation: bring finance and operations leads into the next executive review

## Source basis

This skill is grounded in public enterprise sales, buying committee management, and executive sales enablement practices used by B2B teams selling to complex buying groups.
