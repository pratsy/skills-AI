# Multi-threading Plan Builder

## Purpose

Design a multi-threading strategy for a complex B2B deal so the seller engages all key stakeholders needed to progress the buying process.

## Inputs

- account and buyer list
- stakeholder map
- buying process details
- relationship strengths
- decision roles and influence areas

## Outputs

- stakeholder coverage plan
- buying committee map
- outreach sequence by persona
- risk areas where access is weak
- executive engagement plan

## Prompt

“You are a senior enterprise sales coach and account strategist. Build a multi-threading plan for the account using the stakeholder map and buying process. Identify who to engage, how to sequence outreach, where buying risk is concentrated, and how to increase executive access without creating unnecessary noise.”

## What a strong output includes

- champion and blocker mapping
- economic buyer and technical buyer coverage
- stakeholder-level engagement approach
- suggested timing and message themes
- gaps in current buyer access

## Example result

- Champion identified with strong internal influence
- Missing operational sponsor in finance or implementation
- Recommendation: schedule discovery with security and operations before executive meeting

## Source basis

This skill is informed by public enterprise sales, account planning, and strategic sales frameworks used in B2B deal management and qualification.
