# Multi-threading Plan Builder

## Why this skill exists

Deals stall when the sales team only has one relationship inside the customer.

This skill helps plan stakeholder coverage across the buying group so the team can reduce dependence on a single champion, build influence, and make progress more resilient.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Which additional stakeholders should we engage, and how should we build coverage across the buying group to improve decision quality and reduce risk?

## Expert memory layer

Experienced sales teams know that multi-threading is not just “add more names.” It is about building the right coverage across business, technical, and decision roles.

Patterns that matter include:

- single-threaded deals with hidden decision blockers
- champions with limited authority or influence
- deals that stall when one stakeholder is not aligned
- lack of coverage across different buying criteria and risk concerns

This skill captures those patterns into a practical coverage plan.

## Inputs

- account context and stakeholder map
- buying process and decision structure
- known stakeholders and roles
- existing champion and sponsor strength
- likely blockers or resistance areas

## Decision logic

A strong multi-threading plan should:

1. identify where coverage is weak
2. map the most important buying roles and criteria
3. determine who should be engaged and why
4. prioritize the most strategic coverage moves
5. align each additional stakeholder to a specific business outcome or concern

The goal is strategic coverage, not generic outreach.

## Common failure patterns

- adding contacts with no clear purpose
- ignoring technical and procurement stakeholders
- relying too heavily on one champion
- failing to create a map for business and technical influence
- missing stakeholders who control risk or budget assumptions

## Outputs

- multi-threading coverage plan
- stakeholder role map
- engagement strategy by persona
- key coverage gaps and recommended adds
- actions that improve buying confidence and momentum

## What a strong output includes

- champion and blocker mapping
- economic buyer and technical buyer coverage
- stakeholder-level engagement approach
- suggested timing and message themes
- gaps in current buyer access

## Example result

### Multi-threading plan
- Existing champion: VP of operations
- Priority add: CFO to validate business case and ROI
- Priority add: IT lead to assess implementation and technical compatibility
- Additional coverage: procurement contact to address purchasing flow and governance
- Recommendation: build a tailored message for each role and avoid broad, generic outreach

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior enterprise sales strategist. Review the deal and stakeholder context and build a multi-threading plan that expands coverage across the buying group in a way that reduces risk, improves decision-making, and creates stronger momentum.

## Source basis

This skill is informed by public enterprise sales strategy, stakeholder coverage planning, and buying-group management practices used in complex B2B deals.
