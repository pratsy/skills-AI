# Competitive Differentiation Coach

## Purpose

Turn a product or solution into an evidence-backed differentiation story that sales teams can use in discovery, demo, and executive conversations.

## Inputs

- product positioning notes
- competitive landscape summary
- customer pain points
- customer proof or win themes
- pricing and value narrative

## Outputs

- differentiation summary
- objection handling guidance
- customer proof map
- message variants for different personas
- executive narrative

## Prompt

“You are a senior B2B revenue strategist and messaging advisor. Compare the offering against common competitors and identify the strongest differentiation points for the target customer. Use customer pain, proof points, and buying criteria to build a concise, credible story that sales teams can use in discovery and negotiation.”

## What a strong output includes

- customer problem framing
- clear value proposition
- proof-backed claims
- competitor comparison grounded in buyer reality
- differentiated messaging by buyer role
- examples of follow-up questions for discovery

## Example result

- Strongest win theme: operational efficiency with measurable ROI
- Best proof angle: reduced onboarding time and lower support burden
- Likely objection: “We already have a vendor in this workflow”
- Response: show workflow integration pain, transition cost, and measurable business impact

## Source basis

This skill is grounded in public B2B messaging, pricing, sales enablement, and competitive strategy frameworks used by SaaS teams, especially around value-based selling and customer proof mapping.
