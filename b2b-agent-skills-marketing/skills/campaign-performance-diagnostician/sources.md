### Relevance

This skill reflects common demand generation analysis methods used in B2B marketing optimization and funnel diagnostics.

### Example references

- performance marketing and campaign optimization frameworks
- funnel analysis and attribution practices
- B2B demand generation analysis methods

### Notes

The skill is intended to help teams identify root causes in campaign performance and prioritize the actions that most likely drive improvement.
