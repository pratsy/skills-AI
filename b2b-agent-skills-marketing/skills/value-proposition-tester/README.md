# Value Proposition Tester

## Why this skill exists

A value proposition is only valuable if buyers understand it and believe it.

This skill helps test whether the current value narrative is specific, relevant, and differentiating enough to support buyer interest and conversion.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Does the value proposition connect clearly to the buyer’s real problem, and does it create enough reason to believe to drive action?

## Expert memory layer

Strong positioning teams know that vague statements create noise rather than interest.

Patterns that matter include:

- generic positioning that sounds good internally but not externally
- value claims without proof or buyer relevance
- broad promises that do not map to actual buyer pain
- inconsistent messages across channels or segments

This skill converts these patterns into a structured value proposition review.

## Inputs

- value proposition and messaging framework
- ICP and buyer context
- customer pain points and objections
- competitor or alternative positioning
- conversion and audience response data

## Decision logic

A useful proposition test should examine:

1. clarity of customer problem framing
2. specificity of the outcome or benefit
3. differentiation from alternatives
4. credibility and proof quality
5. relevance for the intended buyer segment or stage

The strongest propositions are easy to understand and easy to believe.

## Common failure patterns

- broad claims with no specific business outcome
- value framing that is too internal or feature-based
- weak proof or reason-to-believe language
- voice that is not aligned with the real buyer language
- inconsistent differentiation across audience segments

## Outputs

- value proposition assessment
- likely gaps in clarity or relevance
- differentiation and proof suggestions
- recommended message edits or reframing
- testing priorities by audience or stage

## Example result

### Proposition challenge
- The value proposition explains the platform but not the customer outcome in concrete terms.
- Recommendation: shift to business outcome positioning and strengthen proof around measurable difference and risk reduction.

> You are a senior positioning strategist. Review the value proposition and buyer context below and assess whether it is clear, relevant, differentiated, and believable. Identify the gaps and suggest the strongest alternative framing.
