# ICP Refinement Agent

## Why this skill exists

An ICP is only useful when it reflects actual buying behavior rather than broad assumptions.

This skill helps marketing and GTM teams refine the ideal customer profile using evidence from buyer fit, conversion patterns, and segment-level performance.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Which customer profiles are truly worth targeting, and which attributes are creating weak fit or wasted effort?

## Expert memory layer

Strong growth teams build memory around the signals that separate real fit from false interest.

Patterns that matter include:

- accounts that look attractive on paper but do not convert
- segments with high volume but low value or poor fit
- buyer behaviors and motion signals that indicate stronger buying intent
- gaps between ICP description and actual customer success patterns

This skill turns those patterns into a clear refinement process.

## Inputs

- current ICP definition
- customer and account data
- sales and marketing conversion patterns
- segment performance and customer outcomes
- buyer behaviors, signals, and objections

## Decision logic

A strong ICP refinement should evaluate:

1. commercial fit and value potential
2. buying behavior and urgency
3. likelihood of successful conversion and retention
4. alignment with the current GTM motion
5. segment-level signal quality and operational fit

The best ICPs reflect the customer profile that actually wins and stays.

## Common failure patterns

- ICPs built from assumptions instead of evidence
- over-indexing on firmographic size without behavioral fit
- treating all good-fit accounts as equally valuable
- ignoring the difference between demand volume and demand quality
- failing to update ICP as buyer behavior changes

## Outputs

- refined ICP summary
- strongest target segments
- weak-fit characteristics to avoid or deprioritize
- recommendation for targeting and messaging shifts
- next actions for marketing and sales alignment

## Example result

### Refined ICP
- Ideal fit: mid-market teams with clear operational pain and a defined buying process
- Strongest buyer attributes: clear business urgency, internal stakeholder alignment, measurable process inefficiency
- Lower-priority fit: organizations with low urgency, fragmented buying process, or weak internal alignment

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior go-to-market strategist. Review the current ICP and customer-performance data and refine the ideal customer profile based on actual buyer fit, conversion patterns, and commercial value. Identify where the current ICP is too broad or misaligned and recommend target segments or adjustments.
