# Customer Journey Friction Audit

## Purpose

Identify where buyers experience friction across the funnel so marketing and sales teams can reduce drop-off, improve conversion, and clarify the next step in the journey.

## Inputs

- funnel conversion data
- landing page and campaign analytics
- website behavior signals
- sales handoff gaps
- lifecycle stage drop-offs

## Outputs

- friction map across funnel stages
- likely reasons for drop-off
- product or messaging friction themes
- conversion opportunity list
- next-step experiments

## Prompt

“You are a senior lifecycle marketing and funnel optimization strategist. Review the customer journey and identify the points where buyers are most likely to stall, hesitate, or disengage. Focus on friction caused by messaging, process, handoff quality, and offer clarity, and recommend prioritized fixes.”

## What a strong output includes

- stage-by-stage drop-off analysis
- suspected blockers and root causes
- practical improvement opportunities
- conversion experiments and messaging guidance
- ownership recommendations across marketing and sales

## Example result

- Friction point: unclear ROI explanation in mid-funnel content
- Recommendation: add calculator and case study proof before sales handoff
- Priority: improve conversion quality for bottom-of-funnel leads

## Source basis

This skill is grounded in public lifecycle marketing, funnel optimization, conversion design, and B2B demand generation frameworks used by SaaS organizations.
