# ABM Account Priority Ranker

## Purpose

Rank target accounts for ABM campaigns based on fit, strategic value, buying readiness, and revenue potential.

## Business problem

Marketing teams often spread effort across too many accounts without enough clarity on which ones deserve personalized programs.

## Inputs

- account list
- firmographic data
- intent signals
- historical revenue potential
- engagement data
- account quality signals

## Outputs

- ranked account list
- reason for ranking
- recommended ABM motion
- suggested channel and content approach

## Use cases

- ABM planning
- quarterly targeting refresh
- strategic campaign planning
- account selection for personalized marketing

## Prompt

“You are a B2B ABM strategist. Rank the following target accounts by strategic value, fit, and buying readiness. Use firmographic fit, engagement intensity, intent signals, and commercial potential. Return a ranked list with rationale and suggested ABM motion for each account.”

## Example output

- Account A: Top priority — high fit, active buyer signals, strong revenue opportunity, personalized outreach recommended
- Account B: Medium priority — good fit but lower urgency, nurture with targeted content and team outreach
- Account C: Lower priority — weak fit, low urgency, no current ABM investment recommended

## Evaluation

- Ranking reflects real target-account quality
- Recommendations vary based on priority level
- Motion type matches account readiness and opportunity value
- Output helps avoid spreading budget thinly across too many accounts
