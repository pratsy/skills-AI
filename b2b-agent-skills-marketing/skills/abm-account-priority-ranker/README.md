# ABM Account Priority Ranker

## Why this skill exists

ABM programs fail when sales and marketing prioritize accounts without enough signal quality or strategic fit.

This skill helps rank accounts for account-based motion by balancing fit, buying urgency, strategic value, and likely conversion potential.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Which accounts deserve focused ABM attention, and which should be deprioritized or nurtured for later?

## Expert memory layer

Strong ABM teams learn that not all accounts with good fit deserve equal attention.

Patterns that matter include:

- accounts with strong fit but weak buying urgency
- high-value accounts with unclear buying readiness
- signal clusters that indicate likely near-term action
- segments where account value is high but internal alignment is weak

This skill turns those patterns into a priority ranking and action plan.

## Inputs

- account data and firmographics
- intent or engagement signals
- buying-stage maturity
- strategic value and conversion potential
- fit against the ICP and target segment

## Decision logic

A strong account prioritization should weigh:

1. fit against the ICP and ideal buyer profile
2. strategic value and commercial potential
3. urgency and engagement momentum
4. likelihood of conversion with the current motion
5. internal sales-readiness and account complexity

The highest priority accounts should have the clearest business fit and strongest decision momentum.

## Common failure patterns

- over-prioritizing large accounts with weak fit
- prioritizing activity rather than readiness
- failing to combine value and urgency in one decision
- treating all ABM accounts as equal when capacity is limited
- missing where signature buying behaviors actually matter

## Outputs

- prioritized account list
- account-level reasoning and fit assessment
- value and urgency summary
- recommended next action by account
- suggested focus tier for ABM efforts

## Example result

### Priority 1
- Strong fit and active buying behavior
- High value and clear urgency
- Recommendation: pursue with personalization and coordinated sales outreach

### Priority 2
- Moderate fit and decent intent but still early in the cycle
- Recommendation: nurture with targeted content and sales follow-up

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior ABM strategist. Review the account and signal data and rank the accounts by strategic value, fit, urgency, and likely conversion quality. Explain why each account deserves attention and recommend the best ABM action for each tier.

This skill is informed by public ABM, account prioritization, and enterprise go-to-market targeting practices used in B2B growth programs.
