# Lifecycle Email Optimizer

## Purpose

Improve lifecycle email sequences so they better support conversion, retention, and buyer progression across the journey.

## Business problem

Lifecycle email strategies often produce generic content that does not reflect buyer stage, value level, or product engagement patterns.

## Inputs

- lifecycle stage map
- audience segment
- engagement behavior
- message goal
- customer stage or conversion stage

## Outputs

- optimized sequence structure
- supporting subject line ideas
- refined CTA logic
- recommended timing and personalization cues

## Use cases

- welcome sequences
- nurturing flows
- product adoption follow-up
- retention or expansion sequences

## Prompt

“You are a B2B lifecycle marketing strategist. Review the provided audience stage and engagement data, then optimize the email sequence to improve conversion or retention. Suggest a better structure, more relevant message focus, and stronger CTAs based on buyer stage and likely next action.”

## Example output

- Sequence adjustment: shift from education-first to value-proof and risk-reduction messaging
- CTA refinement: replace generic CTA with a specific next-step path tied to buying intent
- Timing adjustment: increase engagement frequency for high-intent segments and reduce volume for low-intent segments

## Evaluation

- Sequence reflects actual buyer stage and needs
- CTA and message structure support clearer progression
- The output is adapted for lifecycle rather than mass broadcast use
- It is useful for both conversion and retention motions
