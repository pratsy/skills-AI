# Persona Insight Extractor

## Purpose

Extract the most relevant buyer persona insights from customer or market data and turn them into actionable messaging and targeting guidance.

## Business problem

Many teams treat personas as static demographic descriptions instead of dynamic buying patterns with relevant goals, friction, and business triggers.

## Inputs

- customer interviews
- CRM patterns
- market research
- product adoption patterns
- buyer feedback

## Outputs

- persona summary
- job-to-be-done insight
- key pains and triggers
- decision criteria and objections
- targeting and message guidance

## Use cases

- persona refresh exercises
- market research synthesis
- messaging strategy updates
- campaign segmentation

## Prompt

“You are a B2B persona strategist. Review the provided customer and market data and synthesize the most relevant buyer persona insights. Focus on role, pain, buying triggers, decision criteria, common objections, and ideal message framing.”

## Example output

- Primary persona: mid-market operations leader
- Core pain: fragmented workflow and poor forecasting visibility
- Trigger: scale pressure and operational inconsistency
- Decision criteria: time to value, complexity, and measurable ROI
- Message focus: reduce process friction and increase operating confidence

## Evaluation

- Persona summary reflects real buyer behavior and motivation
- Pain points are specific and actionable
- Output supports stronger targeting and message design
- It moves beyond generic ICP descriptions
