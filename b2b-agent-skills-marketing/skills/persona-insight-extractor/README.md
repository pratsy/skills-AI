# Persona Insight Extractor

## Why this skill exists

A persona is only useful when it reflects how buyers actually think, speak, and make decisions.

This skill helps teams turn customer language, objections, and behavioral patterns into sharper buyer insight for messaging, targeting, and content strategy.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> What do buyers actually care about, what language do they use, and how does that change our targeting and messaging?

## Expert memory layer

Experienced marketers know that buyer insight lives in the patterns behind customer language.

Patterns that matter include:

- repeated pain themes and buying concerns
- language buyers use to describe urgency or risk
- differences in motivation between stakeholders and segments
- the gap between internal product language and customer language

This skill captures those patterns into a more actionable buyer model.

## Inputs

- customer interviews and notes
- conversations, objections, and sales feedback
- buyer segments and account data
- messaging and campaign language
- customer support or product feedback signals

## Decision logic

A useful persona insight review should identify:

1. the real buyer pain and buying motivation
2. where the buyer sees risk or effort
3. which language is most relevant and credible
4. what different buyer roles care about most
5. what this means for messaging and targeting

The goal is to make persona insight operational, not theoretical.

## Common failure patterns

- personas built from assumptions rather than lived buyer language
- too much focus on demographics and not enough on buying behavior
- treating the persona as one uniform buyer instead of multiple stakeholder perspectives
- failing to reflect the language buyers actually use in the market

## Outputs

- buyer insight summary
- common pain themes and buying motivations
- stakeholder-specific concerns and language patterns
- recommended messaging implications
- targeting or content recommendations

## Example result

### Persona insight
- Buyers care most about operational efficiency, integration burden, and visibility across teams.
- Their language is more operational and outcome-driven than product-driven.
- Recommendation: shift messaging toward business outcomes and practical implementation confidence.

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior buyer-insight analyst. Review the customer language, objections, and buying behavior below and identify the most important persona patterns, motivations, and message implications for the team.

## Source basis

This skill is informed by public buyer insight, audience analysis, and customer-language work used in strong B2B marketing organizations.
