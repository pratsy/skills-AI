## Attribution Model Reviewer

### Relevance

This skill reflects public models and practices used for channel attribution, multi-touch analysis, and sales and marketing measurement design.

### Example references

- attribution modeling in digital and B2B marketing
- multi-touch measurement and channel analysis frameworks
- pipeline-to-channel reporting methods in SaaS GTM programs
- sales and marketing KPI governance practices

### Notes

The objective is to improve measurement realism and avoid decision-making based on flawed or oversimplified attribution logic.
