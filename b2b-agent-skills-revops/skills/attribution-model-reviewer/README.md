# Attribution Model Reviewer

## Why this skill exists

Attribution is often treated like a reporting feature rather than a decision system.

This skill helps evaluate whether the current attribution model is credible, reliable enough for decision-making, and aligned with the real customer journey and sales motion.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Is the attribution model helping us understand what is driving growth, or is it creating false confidence in weak or incomplete signals?

## Expert memory layer

Experienced RevOps teams know that attribution quality depends on the structure of the customer journey and the validity of the data.

Patterns that matter include:

- channels that look strong because they drive early discovery but do not generate quality opportunities
- models that over-credit touchpoints without enough causal evidence
- attribution assumptions that do not reflect the real buying path or multi-touch behavior
- measurement systems that are easy to report but hard to trust for operational decisions

This skill captures those patterns into a structured review.

## Inputs

- attribution model and channel data
- customer journey and touchpoint history
- source and campaign mix
- conversion or opportunity creation data
- sales and marketing alignment context

## Decision logic

A strong attribution review should evaluate:

1. whether the model reflects the real customer journey
2. whether the channel logic is fair and operationally useful
3. whether conversion and opportunity quality are being correctly weighed
4. whether the model supports real budget decisions
5. whether there are blind spots or misattribution patterns

A good attribution model helps teams decide where to invest, not just what to report.

## Common failure patterns

- crediting the last touch without understanding the importance of earlier signals
- treating channel performance as equal across segments and motions
- using incomplete data to create false certainty
- failing to examine conversion quality versus volume generation
- building reporting models that look precise but are not decision-ready

## Outputs

- attribution model assessment
- likely model weaknesses or blind spots
- channel quality and credibility findings
- recommendation for model adjustment or monitoring
- decision support guidance for marketing and RevOps teams

## Example result

### Attribution issue
- A channel appears strong due to early engagement volume, but it is not producing high-quality opportunities or strong downstream conversion.
- Recommendation: review the channel weighting and add stronger quality filters or conversion signals to the model.

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior RevOps and measurement strategist. Review the attribution model and the customer journey data below and identify where the model is likely misrepresenting channel performance or buying momentum. Recommend how to improve the model so it supports actual decisions.
