# Attribution Model Reviewer

## Purpose

Review attribution logic and identify where measurement design may be misrepresenting the true contribution of channels or stages.

## Business problem

Attribution models are often overly simple or poorly aligned with actual buying behavior. This creates distorted optimization decisions and misleading performance views.

## Inputs

- attribution model details
- channel mix
- sales cycle and funnel data
- conversion points
- reporting logic

## Outputs

- attribution quality assessment
- likely blind spots
- recommended modeling adjustments
- measurement limitations summary

## Use cases

- performance reporting review
- channel optimization diagnostics
- strategy reviews
- marketing and sales KPI governance

## Prompt

“You are a revenue analytics reviewer. Assess the current attribution model for blind spots, over-attribution risks, and misalignment with real buyer behavior. Explain the likely issues and recommend a more credible measurement approach for this funnel and channel mix.”

## Example output

- The model likely over-attributes last touch and under-values earlier research and sales influence
- High-risk blind spot: channel and content interactions are not modeled properly across multi-touch motions
- Recommendation: add a multi-touch or blended view, especially in longer B2B sales cycles

## Evaluation

- Findings are grounded in real measurement logic
- Weaknesses are clearly explained and action-oriented
- Recommendations are realistic for business reporting and channel planning
- Output supports better strategic and channel decisions
