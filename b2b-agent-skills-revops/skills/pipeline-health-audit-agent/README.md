# Pipeline Health Audit Agent

## Purpose

Assess pipeline quality across stages, identify where deals are stalling, and highlight operational issues that reduce forecast accuracy and conversion efficiency.

## Inputs

- CRM pipeline data
- stage movement history
- deal age and velocity
- sales leader commentary
- conversion and win-rate benchmarks

## Outputs

- pipeline health score
- stage-specific bottlenecks
- likely forecast risk areas
- account-level intervention suggestions
- recommended RevOps actions

## Prompt

“You are a senior RevOps strategist and sales operations analyst. Review the pipeline for quality, stage health, conversion velocity, and forecast reliability. Identify the biggest blockers to pipeline progression, highlight risk patterns, and recommend operational fixes for the sales team and forecasting process.”

## What a strong output includes

- stage-by-stage conversion analysis
- aging and velocity diagnostics
- suspected gaps in qualification or handoff quality
- risk clusters by segment or rep
- specific leadership actions for the next review cycle

## Example result

- Stage risk: too many deals stalling in late-stage discovery
- Root pattern: weak qualification and unclear next-step ownership
- Recommendation: tighten discovery criteria and require decision criteria before advancing deals

## Source basis

This skill is informed by public revenue operations, pipeline governance, forecasting, and sales process management frameworks widely used in SaaS and B2B organizations.
