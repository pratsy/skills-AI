# Pipeline Health Audit Agent

## Why this skill exists

A pipeline can look large and still be unhealthy.

This skill helps teams audit the quality of the pipeline, identify which stages or motions are structurally weak, and surface the operational issues that are reducing revenue confidence.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Is the pipeline healthy enough to support a reliable forecast and efficient sales execution?

## Expert memory layer

Experienced RevOps teams know that healthy pipelines are built on quality, stage realism, and clear progression patterns.

Patterns that matter include:

- deals sitting too long without meaningful progression
- weak conversion between high-intent stages
- sales motions that create volume without quality
- qualification patterns that inflate pipeline coverage without reducing risk

This skill translates that knowledge into a clear audit format.

## Inputs

- stage-by-stage pipeline data
- conversion metrics and stage aging
- source or motion mix
- deal quality or sales qualification signals
- forecast or pipeline review notes

## Decision logic

A strong pipeline audit should assess:

1. stage balance and progression realism
2. conversion efficiency by source and segment
3. pipeline quality versus pipeline volume
4. risk of overstatement or false confidence
5. the operational changes that would improve forecast quality

The most useful review is not simply a volume diagnosis, but a quality diagnosis.

## Common failure patterns

- treating pipeline size as pipeline health
- overlooking poor stage-to-stage conversion quality
- ignoring source or process issues that create weak deals
- analyzing activity instead of buying progression
- failing to identify operational bottlenecks before review time

## Outputs

- pipeline health summary
- weak-stage and risk findings
- operational and qualification issues
- recommended improvements for quality and forecast confidence
- action priorities for RevOps and sales leadership

## Example result

### Audit finding
- Pipeline volume is healthy, but stage conversion is weakening in the transition from discovery to validation.
- A significant share of deals are stuck without clear progression or buying signal.
- Recommendation: tighten qualification discipline and review handoff quality between SDR and AE teams.

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior RevOps analyst. Audit the pipeline quality and identify the operational issues affecting conversion, forecast confidence, and revenue health. Focus on stage realism, progression quality, and the actions that would improve the health of the pipeline.
