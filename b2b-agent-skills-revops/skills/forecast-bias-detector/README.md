# Forecast Bias Detector

## Why this skill exists

Forecasts are often biased by optimism, recent performance, or a lack of stage discipline.

This skill helps teams detect when the forecast is overly confident, chronically optimistic, or distorted by weak qualification patterns.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Is the forecast being inflated by bias or is it grounded in realistic buyer progression and deal quality?

## Expert memory layer

Experienced RevOps leaders know that forecast bias is usually visible in patterns:

- overconfident stage assignments
- leader optimism not supported by stage quality
- source or segment patterns that consistently underperform expectations
- deals that are counted as likely wins before real buying evidence is strong

This skill captures those patterns in a structured review.

## Inputs

- stage-level pipeline data
- forecast confidence notes
- opportunity quality and progression signals
- historical forecast accuracy by segment or rep
- recent forecast revisions and late-stage volatility

## Decision logic

A strong bias review should examine:

1. whether the pipeline reflects actual buying progress
2. whether confidence levels are aligned with evidence
3. whether certain segments or motions are repeatedly overestimated
4. whether stage discipline is being applied consistently
5. what operational changes would improve forecast realism

Good forecasting depends on disciplined evidence, not optimism.

## Common failure patterns

- over-crediting early stage deals that have weak evidence
- ignoring historical bias by rep, segment, or source
- treating all opportunities as equally likely to close
- minimizing forecast risk because the pipeline is full
- poor stage quality and weak qualification disguised as confidence

## Outputs

- forecast bias summary
- likely overstatement or understatement patterns
- source or segment trend review
- recommended adjustments for stage confidence and review discipline
- next steps for sales and RevOps leadership

## Example result

### Bias finding
- Forecast confidence is inflated in a specific segment where stage quality is weak and close rates are lower than historical assumptions.
- Recommendation: adjust confidence and tighten stage-entry standards before final forecast review.

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior RevOps forecasting analyst. Review the forecast and pipeline data and identify any evidence of bias, overstatement, or weak stage quality. Explain what is driving the bias and recommend practical adjustments.
