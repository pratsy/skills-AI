# Pipeline Health Monitor

## Purpose

Assess the health of a pipeline by identifying weak stages, coverage gaps, and risk patterns before they affect revenue outcomes.

## Business problem

Revenue teams often do not have a clear view of whether the pipeline is healthy, balanced, or vulnerable to slippage.

## Inputs

- stage-by-stage pipeline data
- conversion metrics
- time-to-close trends
- source mix
- deal quality signals

## Outputs

- pipeline health assessment
- weak stage summary
- risk indicators
- recommended operational fixes

## Use cases

- weekly revenue meeting
- sales ops review
- forecast planning
- pipeline maintenance

## Prompt

“You are a revenue operations analyst. Evaluate the pipeline health using the provided stage conversion and deal data. Identify weak stages, conversion problems, and potential risk areas. Provide a concise assessment and recommend operational actions to improve health and forecasting reliability.”

## Example output

- Pipeline health: moderate
- Weak stage: qualification to proposal conversion is underperforming
- Risk: too many deals are sitting in early stages without clear progression
- Action: tighten qualification criteria and improve handoff quality between SDR and AE teams

## Evaluation

- Assessment is grounded in pipeline behavior
- Recommendations are operationally practical
- Output helps both GTM execution and forecasting quality
