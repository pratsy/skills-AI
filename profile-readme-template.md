# B2B AI for Revenue Teams

I build AI playbooks for B2B sales, marketing, revenue operations, and go-to-market teams.

## What I work on

I focus on the operational layers of revenue execution that matter most in modern GTM:

- sales qualification and discovery
- deal risk and forecasting
- pipeline health and RevOps quality
- ICP and messaging refinement
- ABM and lifecycle optimization
- campaign performance and conversion strategy
- competitive and market intelligence

## Featured repos

- `b2b-agent-skills` — flagship library and shared taxonomy
- `b2b-agent-skills-sales` — sales workflows, objections, forecasting, and commercial optimization
- `b2b-agent-skills-marketing` — messaging, lifecycle, ABM, and conversion strategies
- `b2b-agent-skills-revops` — pipeline quality, attribution, forecasting, and operational health
- `b2b-agent-skills-gtm` — strategic intelligence, market scanning, and prioritization

## Why this project exists

Most AI projects are generic copilots.

This one is different: it is built around the real operating motions of B2B revenue teams, with a focus on repeatability, business context, and execution quality.

## What’s inside

- structured skill definitions

## Positioning

Built as a practitioner-informed, operationally grounded B2B GTM skill library rather than a raw prompt dump or experimental notebook.

## Open source intent

The goal is to create a useful, extensible library of AI playbooks for teams that want better structure, higher signal, and more business relevance in their GTM workflows.
