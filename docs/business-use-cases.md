# Business use cases for non-technical users

This guide shows how a business person can use the repo without reading the Python runtime or repo internals.

## 1. Sales leader: prioritize accounts that deserve attention

Problem:
"We have too many accounts and not enough clarity on where to focus."

Prompt:
"Review the list of accounts below and rank them by strategic priority, buying urgency, fit, and likely commercial value. For each account, explain why it deserves attention now or later, and suggest the next best sales action."

What this gives:
- prioritized account list
- explicit reasoning
- action suggestions by account
- clearer sales focus

## 2. Marketing lead: find why campaign performance is weak

Problem:
"Traffic is strong, but conversion is weak and we do not know why."

Prompt:
"Analyze the campaign data, funnel stages, and content themes below. Identify the most likely bottlenecks in the customer journey, the content or message gaps causing friction, and the highest-priority opportunities to improve conversion."

What this gives:
- funnel bottleneck analysis
- content and messaging recommendations
- clear opportunity list

## 3. RevOps leader: improve pipeline and forecast quality

Problem:
"Our pipeline looks full but forecast confidence is inconsistent."

Prompt:
"Review the current pipeline data and identify stage health issues, forecast risk, and likely sources of bias. Highlight which deals are likely overstated, which segments are underperforming, and what operational changes would improve forecast quality."

What this gives:
- pipeline health assessment
- forecast bias view
- operational recommendations

## 4. GTM operator: understand market and competitor movement

Problem:
"We are seeing signals, but not enough clarity on what matters strategically."

Prompt:
"Review the market, competitor, and customer signal data below. Identify the strongest market shifts, the most important competitor themes, and the business implications for our GTM positioning and account strategy."

What this gives:
- market signal summary
- strategic implication analysis
- recommended GTM response

## 5. Founder: reduce decision noise and align teams

Problem:
"We have lots of data but slow decisions and unclear priorities."

Prompt:
"Summarize the recent sales, marketing, and customer signal data and identify the most important business issues, the highest-value opportunities, and the actions that should be prioritized in the next 30 days."

What this gives:
- concise strategic snapshot
- fewer decision blind spots
- aligned priorities across functions

## Typical business outcomes

Using these skills can help a team:
- reduce wasted time on low-priority work
- improve decision quality
- align GTM motions around the same metrics
- fix revenue bottlenecks earlier
- make the next move more obvious

## Recommended workflow

1. Pick the business problem
2. Collect the relevant context
3. Run a skill or prompt
4. Review the result with your team
5. Turn it into a next-step action plan

The point is not to replace judgment. The point is to shorten the gap between signal and action.
