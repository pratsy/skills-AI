# Prompt packs for business teams

These are ready-to-use prompt patterns for non-technical people. They are written so a sales lead, marketer, RevOps person, or founder can use the repo without needing to understand the code.

## 1. Sales leader prompt pack

### Account prioritization prompt

> Review the account list below and rank each account by strategic value, urgency, buying signal strength, and likely conversion quality. Identify which accounts deserve immediate attention, which should be nurtured, and which should be deprioritized. For each account, explain your reasoning and suggest the next sales action.

### Deal review prompt

> Review the active deals below and identify the deals most likely to stall, the likely reason for risk, the missing stakeholder signals, and the best next action for the seller or account team.

## 2. Marketing lead prompt pack

### Messaging and positioning prompt

> Review the current product messaging, customer objections, and recent campaign language. Identify the biggest message gaps, common buyer confusion, and the highest-value improvements to positioning and content strategy.

### Funnel friction prompt

> Analyze the funnel stages and conversion drop-off points below. Identify which stage is losing the most value, what is causing friction, and which content, offers, or process changes would improve conversion quality.

## 3. RevOps prompt pack

### Pipeline health prompt

> Review the recent pipeline data and identify the biggest stage health issues, deals likely to miss forecast, and the biggest operational causes of pipeline weakness. Recommend changes to improve forecast confidence and handoff quality.

### Forecast review prompt

> Examine the forecast and stage movement data below. Identify whether the forecast shows optimism or conservatism, which segments are over- or under-estimated, and what actions would improve forecast quality in the next cycle.

## 4. GTM operator prompt pack

### Market shift prompt

> Review the market, competitor, and customer signal data below. Identify the strongest changes in category behavior, the most important competitor themes, and the likely strategic implications for messaging, positioning, and account focus.

### Account strategy prompt

> Build an account priority matrix using fit, urgency, revenue potential, and strategic alignment. Separate the accounts into clear tiers and recommend how sales, marketing, and partnerships should allocate effort across them.

## 5. Founder prompt pack

### 30-day operating review prompt

> Summarize the recent sales, marketing, and customer data below. Identify the top issues affecting growth, the highest-value opportunities, and the actions that should be prioritized in the next 30 days to improve momentum.

## How to use these

1. Pick the prompt that matches your current problem.
2. Add the relevant business context or data.
3. Run it through your AI tool of choice, or through `skills_ai` if it's one of the five skills wired into the SDK (see the root [README](README.md)).
4. Review the result with your team before acting on it — these prompts are a starting point, not a substitute for judgment on a specific deal or account.

For the fuller, formula-driven version of any of these (explicit scoring model, worked example, output schema), go to the matching skill's README in the relevant pack — these quick prompts are a faster on-ramp, not a replacement for it.
