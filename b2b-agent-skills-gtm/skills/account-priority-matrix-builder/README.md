# Account Priority Matrix Builder

## Purpose

Create a structured prioritization model that helps GTM teams decide which accounts deserve the most attention based on fit, urgency, expansion potential, and strategic value.

## Inputs

- ICP and target segment data
- account fit signals
- buying intent information
- revenue potential estimates
- strategic alignment or partner relevance

## Outputs

- account priority matrix
- segment-level recommendations
- coverage strategy by segment
- outreach priority sequence
- strategic focus areas

## Prompt

“You are a senior GTM strategist and revenue planner. Build an account priority matrix for the target segment using fit, buying urgency, commercial potential, and strategic value. Rank accounts into clear tiers and recommend how GTM teams should allocate effort across sales, marketing, and partner motions.”

## What a strong output includes

- prioritized account tiering
- rationale for each tier
- coverage recommendation by team or motion
- highest-leverage strategic accounts
- next-step sequencing for sales and marketing ownership

## Example result

- Tier 1: high-fit, strong intent, highest expansion potential
- Tier 2: promising but not urgent
- Tier 3: low-priority or poor strategic fit

## Source basis

This skill is informed by public GTM planning, account segmentation, ABM prioritization, and strategic account planning frameworks used by modern B2B growth teams.
