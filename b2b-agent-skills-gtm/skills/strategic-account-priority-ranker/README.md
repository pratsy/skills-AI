# Strategic Account Priority Ranker

## Purpose

Identify the accounts that deserve the strongest strategic attention based on growth potential, fit, and urgency.

## Business problem

Not every account deserves the same investment. Teams need a disciplined way to decide where strategic focus should go.

## Inputs

- account list
- fit data
- intent and engagement signals
- revenue potential
- competitive risk
- current stage and urgency

## Outputs

- strategic account ranking
- recommended focus level
- reasons for priority
- suggested motion for each account

## Use cases

- annual strategic planning
- executive account review
- ABM expansion
- resource allocation for top accounts

## Prompt

“You are a strategic GTM advisor. Rank the following accounts by strategic importance, fit, buying urgency, and commercial upside. Identify which accounts deserve the strongest strategic attention and recommend the likely GTM motion for each one.”

## Example output

- Top priority: high strategic value, strong fit, clear urgency and strong buying signal
- Secondary priority: good fit but slower buying cycle, requires careful nurture and value proof
- Lower priority: low strategic value or weak fit, maintain passive monitoring only

## Evaluation

- Ranking balances commercial value with buyer readiness
- Recommendations are realistic and focused
- Strategic coverage and resource allocation logic are clear
- Output supports executive GTM prioritization decisions
