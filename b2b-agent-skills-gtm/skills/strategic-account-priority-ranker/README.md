# Strategic Account Priority Ranker

## Why this skill exists

Some accounts deserve attention because they are valuable and strategically important, even if they are not easiest to win.

This skill helps rank strategic accounts by business importance, fit, buying momentum, and GTM feasibility so leadership can focus attention on the highest-value opportunities.

## Business objective

This skill helps the team answer:

> Which strategic accounts are worth the most attention now, and how should the GTM team prioritize them?

## Expert memory layer

Experienced GTM teams know that strategic value and sales ease are not the same thing.

Patterns that matter include:

- accounts with strong strategic value but slow buying momentum
- accounts with high fit but lower commercial leverage
- segments where strategic attention is likely to reshape the portfolio
- actions where the team can create leverage for expansion or ecosystem value

This skill captures those patterns into a prioritized strategic list.

## Inputs

- account strategic value signals
- segment fit and commercial potential
- deal maturity and buying momentum
- growth opportunity and expansion value
- sales and marketing capability constraints

## Decision logic

A strong strategic ranking should weigh:

1. strategic value and revenue potential
2. account fit and buying readiness
3. urgency and market timing
4. execution difficulty or internal complexity
5. the potential for creating broader account leverage

The result should show where strategic attention will have the highest return for the business.

## Common failure patterns

- ranking only by account size without strategic logic
- focusing on easy wins while ignoring high-leverage opportunities
- not considering account complexity or execution burden
- over-prioritizing thin opportunities that are not strategically meaningful

## Outputs

- strategic priority list
- rank by account value and actionability
- recommendation for GTM attention level
- rationale for why the account deserves focus
- next-step guidance for leadership and sales teams

## Example result

### Strategic priority ranking
- Priority 1: high-value account with real urgency and good fit
- Priority 2: high-value account with medium urgency but broader strategic leverage
- Priority 3: low urgency but strong strategic value; monitor and nurture

## Recommended prompt

> You are a senior GTM strategist. Rank the strategic accounts by value, fit, urgency, and operational feasibility. Focus on where leadership attention will create the greatest business leverage and where the team should invest effort first.
