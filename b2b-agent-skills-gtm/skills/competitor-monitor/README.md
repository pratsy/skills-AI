# Competitor Monitor

## Purpose

Track and summarize competitor movements, positioning changes, and strategic signals that may affect GTM decisions.

## Business problem

Sales and marketing teams often miss the strategic signals that matter most: competitor positioning shifts, pricing changes, launch activity, and buying narratives that are evolving in market.

## Inputs

- competitor websites
- product announcements
- pricing changes
- content trends
- customer feedback
- market conversations

## Outputs

- competitor summary
- strategic shifts
- likely impact on buying behavior
- recommended sales or marketing response

## Use cases

- market intelligence briefing
- sales battlecard refresh
- account strategy support
- GTM planning reviews

## Prompt

“You are a B2B market intelligence analyst. Review the provided competitor information and summarize what has changed strategically, what messaging is shifting, and what likely effect this will have on buyer behavior and our GTM motion. Provide a concise briefing and recommendations for sales and marketing.”

## Example output

- Competitor shift: repositioning to emphasize faster implementation and lower operational risk
- Likely buyer impact: customers now compare implementation speed and integration effort more heavily
- Recommended response: strengthen proof points around deployment speed and lower effort, update sales objection handling

## Evaluation

- Findings are grounded in actual competitor activity
- Implications are clear and actionable
- Output helps both sales and marketing teams respond strategically
