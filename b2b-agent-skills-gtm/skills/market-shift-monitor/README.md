# Market Shift Monitor

## Purpose

Track signals of market change, competitor movement, and buyer behavior shifts so GTM teams can adapt messaging, pacing, and strategic focus before the market moves too far.

## Inputs

- market trend reports
- competitor announcements
- customer conversation themes
- pricing or packaging changes
- campaign and intent data

## Outputs

- market shift summary
- competitor movement summary
- segment-level change signals
- recommended repositioning or messaging adjustments
- strategic response options

## Prompt

“You are a senior market intelligence strategist. Review the incoming signals across market dynamics, competitor behavior, and buyer sentiment. Identify meaningful shifts, explain why they matter, and recommend GTM responses across messaging, targeting, and positioning.”

## What a strong output includes

- trend detection and confidence level
- explanation of likely buyer impact
- competitor or category movement summary
- recommended GTM response by channel or persona
- risk and opportunity framing for leadership

## Example result

- Shift: consolidation in the category creates urgency around integration and ROI proof
- Recommendation: reposition around operational efficiency and interoperability
- Priority audience: security-conscious mid-market buyers with limited implementation bandwidth

## Source basis

This skill is grounded in public market intelligence, competitive monitoring, category analysis, and B2B strategy frameworks used by revenue and growth organizations.
