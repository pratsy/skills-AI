# Market Sizing Modeler

## Purpose

Estimate the addressable opportunity, commercial size, and strategic relevance of a segment or market for GTM planning.

## Business problem

Teams often plan around vague market assumptions instead of a structured estimate of opportunity and strategic attractiveness.

## Inputs

- market description
- segment definition
- customer count or revenue proxies
- historical market patterns
- buyer profile and buying power signals

## Outputs

- market size estimate
- target segment summary
- strategic opportunity view
- recommended prioritization logic

## Use cases

- new market exploration
- strategic planning
- product market fit review
- GTM prioritization

## Prompt

“You are a market strategy analyst. Estimate the commercial opportunity for this target market using the provided data and assumptions. Explain the size logic, key drivers, and any uncertainty or limitations. Return a concise but strategic estimate with implications for GTM planning.”

## Example output

- Total addressable segment: moderate to high
- Key drivers: customer volume, average contract value, and software adoption patterns
- Strategic implication: attractive for targeted GTM motion but requires differentiation and sustained outbound execution

## Evaluation

- Estimate is grounded in reasonable assumptions
- Key drivers and uncertainty are transparent
- Output is useful for strategic planning and resource decisions
- It supports GTM prioritization rather than speculation
