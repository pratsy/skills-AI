# Discovery Question Generator

## Purpose

Generate relevant, buyer-focused discovery questions for sales calls and qualification conversations.

## Business problem

Reps often ask generic questions and miss key buyer context such as urgency, stakeholders, and internal process.

## Inputs

- account summary
- buyer persona
- challenge description
- stage of buying process
- industry context

## Outputs

- tailored discovery questions
- prioritized question flow
- stakeholder insight prompts

## Use cases

- discovery calls
- enterprise qualification
- multi-stakeholder discovery
- deal prep

## Prompt

“You are a senior B2B sales strategist. Generate a high-quality discovery question set for a conversation with a [persona]. The buyer is dealing with [challenge]. Focus on pain, urgency, process, decision criteria, and stakeholder alignment. Keep questions open-ended and buyer-centric.”

## Example output

- What changed recently that made this issue more urgent?
- What is the current impact on team productivity or revenue?
- Who is involved in the buying decision?
- What would success look like in the next 6 months?
- What alternatives are you evaluating?

## Evaluation

- Questions reveal business pain
- Questions open up stakeholder and decision process
- Questions are not generic or salesy
